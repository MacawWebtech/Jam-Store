# Image sources and prompts

## Original AI-created lifestyle scenes

Created with the built-in OpenAI imagegen tool for this fictional brand. All three originals were generated as 1536 × 1024 landscapes, inspected and optimized to local WebP. There are no third-party stock photographs in this package.

- `assets/images/hero.webp`: Editorial homemade strawberry jam in clear unlabeled jars, fresh strawberries and toast on a warm rustic kitchen table with cream linen, gentle window daylight, berry and cream palette, no branding/text/watermarks.
- `assets/images/orchard.webp`: Countryside fruit orchard at golden morning light, ripe peaches on branches and a wicker harvest basket, natural farm-to-jar storytelling, no text/branding/watermarks.
- `assets/images/gift.webp`: Elegant artisan gift hamper, open kraft box with three different fruit jam jars, plain cream labels without text, twine and fruit on a cream stone table, natural green details, restrained premium still life, no branding/watermarks.

Intentional crops derived from these same brand scenes are used for breakfast, harvest and packaging stories. They are not presented as photographs of an actual business.

## Original authored illustrations

Product jar art, botanical SVGs, gift-set illustrations, maker portraits, kitchen/editorial illustrations, missing-jar scene and logo were authored specifically for this template. Jar labels and product/team information are fictional. The makers are explicitly illustrated demo profiles. Replace these assets with real verified product and team photography when preparing an actual shop.

DejaVu Sans/Serif glyphs were rendered into illustration assets; web typography uses system fonts and does not require remote Google Fonts requests.
